Agrotech Scenario

Optimizing Harumanis Growth in Perlis

In precision Malaysian agriculture, managing the signature Harumanis mango (MA 128) requires meticulous scheduling. Using empirical data recorded by the Malaysian Agricultural Research and Development Institute (MARDI), fruit growth follows a classic biological Sigmoid curve (logistic growth).

Data Modeling Sources:

  • MARDI Harumanis Perlis Growth Database (2022)
  • Manual Penanaman Mangga Harumanis - Jabatan Pertanian Negeri Perlis

The Mathematical Model

The growth of the mango fruit mass $f(x)$ over $x$ (Weeks After Fruit Set, WAFS) is modeled using a sigmoid curve. The derivative $f'(x)$ models the instantaneous growth rate (grams/week).

Growth Function f(x) Sigmoid Curve

f(x) = 450 / (1 + e-0.6(x - 7))

Where 450g is the maximum potential ripe mass of a premium Harumanis, and growth speed peaks at 7 weeks.

Derivative f'(x) Instantaneous Rate of Change

f'(x) = 270e-0.6(x - 7) / (1 + e-0.6(x - 7))2

Represents the instantaneous speed of weight gain (g/week). Obtained using the Quotient Rule and Chain Rule.
